I’m Dee Sanas, a technology leader, Solutions Architect, and Project Manager with more than a decade of experience helping organizations design and deliver secure, scalable, and high-performing technology solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of business priorities and technical reality. I focus on practical architecture, disciplined delivery, and decisions that create measurable value rather than complexity for its own sake.

How I approach the work

  1. Start with the outcomeArchitecture should connect directly to the customer, operational, or business result.
  2. Make trade-offs visibleGood decisions come from clearly understanding cost, risk, complexity, and time.
  3. Design for operationsA solution is only successful when teams can run, secure, and evolve it reliably.
  4. Communicate with clarityComplexity belongs in the system, not in the explanation.
